How it works

Step 1

Run the job on site

Pick the restaurant, walk the hood checklist, and attach compressed before/after photos per area from the phone camera.

Step 2

Generate the service record

Download a PDF with areas cleaned, deficiency notes, photo grid, and next due date based on cooking volume. Email a summary with one tap.

Step 3

Rebook from one board

See every account by last service and next due. Overdue accounts float to the top with a prefilled rebook note you can copy or send.
