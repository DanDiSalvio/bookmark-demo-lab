Built for office managers at small prime contractors on Davis-Bacon / federally funded jobs who collect weekly certified payroll from subs — and need to spot kickback-worthy defects before forwarding to the contracting agency.

How it works

  1. 01

    Drop in the sub's WH-347 PDF

    Upload each subcontractor's weekly certified payroll. We extract text client-side and run heuristic checks — form revision markers, wage determination number, apprentice registration, fringe benefits, and signed Statement of Compliance.

  2. 02

    See pass/fail on your project grid

    Your project view is a grid of subs by week. Green means ready to forward; red means kick it back before the agency rejects it and treats it like a missing filing.

  3. 03

    Copy the kickback note

    One click copies a professional note listing exactly what the sub needs to fix — wrong revision, missing WD number, unsigned compliance statement, and more.

Heuristic checks — not legal advice

Checks scan PDF text for markers like OMB 1235-0008 (current WH-347 expires 01/31/2028 per DOL), wage determination numbers, apprentice registration, fringe benefit breakdowns, and signed Statements of Compliance. Results are heuristic — always verify before withholding payment or rejecting a filing.
