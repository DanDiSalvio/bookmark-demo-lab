How it works

  1. 01

    Drop in the sub's WH-347 PDF

    Upload each subcontractor's weekly certified payroll. Text is extracted in your browser and checked for form version, wage determination number, apprentice registration, fringe benefits, and a signed Statement of Compliance.

  2. 02

    See status on your project grid

    Your project view is a grid of subs by week. Ready means forward; issues means kick it back before the agency rejects it and treats it like a missing filing.

  3. 03

    Send the correction message

    Copy or open a ready-to-send note listing what the sub needs to fix — wrong form version, missing WD number, unsigned compliance statement, and more.
