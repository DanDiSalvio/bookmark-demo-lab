How it works

  1. 1

    Miss the call

    A customer rings your business line while you're under a sink or on a roof. No answer — they're already dialing the next name on Google.

  2. 2

    Lookup + auto-text

    We reverse-lookup the number (area, carrier, caller ID) and send a personalized SMS with your booking link — new leads get the pitch, returning customers get a shorter message.

  3. 3

    Lead card in your inbox

    You get a lead card with phone, lookup summary, known/new badge, and SMS status — so you can follow up when you're off the ladder.

How production works

Your business number forwards through a service like Twilio or OpenPhone. When a call is missed or goes unanswered, a webhook triggers the text-back — this demo simulates that flow. We never read your phone's call log; browsers can't access iOS or Android call history.
